5.SUITABILITY

## Suitability for Shop Didi

1. DOCUMENT MODEL (MongoDB) - BEST FIT:
- Reason: Shop Didi deals with custom measurements. Each customer has different measurements (bust, waist, extra_length). In SQL this would need many nullable columns or extra tables. In NoSQL we embed measurements as flexible JSON.
- Example: is_custom=true products need custom_measurements field only when needed.
- Reviews embedded in products = faster product page load, no JOIN.

2. KEY-VALUE - Good for Cart & Sessions:
- Fast lookup for cart: customer_id -> cart items. Expires easily.

3. COLUMN FAMILY - Good for Analytics:
- Shop Didi needs to analyze sales by fabric_type, category, designer performance over time.

4. GRAPH - Good for Recommendations:
- "Customers who bought this Agbada also bought this Kaftan" and "Designers similar to Zee Atelier".

CONCLUSION: Shop Didi is hybrid. Core catalogue + orders = Document model. Cart = Key-Value. Recommendation = Graph.
